⚡ Ohm’s Law

📌 Definition

Ohm’s Law states that voltage is directly proportional to current and resistance is constant.

📌 Formula

V = I × R

📌 Explanation

  • V = Voltage (Volt)
  • I = Current (Ampere)
  • R = Resistance (Ohm)

📌 Important Points

  • Used in all electrical circuits
  • Basic law of electricity
  • Helps in circuit calculation
