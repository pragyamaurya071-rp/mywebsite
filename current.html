🔌 Electric Current

Basic Electricity - Chapter 1

📖 Definition

Electric current is the rate of flow of electric charge through a conductor.

🖼 Figure

⚡ Formula

I = Q / t

📌 SI Unit

Ampere (A)

🔹 Symbol

I

💡 Applications

  • Electric Fan
  • LED Bulb
  • Electric Iron
  • Water Pump
  • Mobile Charger

📝 Important Points

  • Current always flows in a closed circuit.
  • SI Unit is Ampere.
  • Measured by Ammeter.
  • Symbol of current is I.
