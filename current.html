⚡ Kirchhoff’s Current Law (KCL)

📌 Definition

According to KCL, the total current entering a junction is equal to the total current leaving the junction.

📌 Statement

Sum of incoming currents = Sum of outgoing currents

📌 Formula

∑I(in) = ∑I(out)

📌 Explanation

  • Based on conservation of charge
  • Used in node analysis

📌 Uses

  • Circuit analysis
  • Finding unknown currents
